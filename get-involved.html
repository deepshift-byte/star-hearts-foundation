Questions people ask

How do I pay?

Choose an amount above or message us. We will reply on WhatsApp with mobile money and bank details.

Can I sponsor more than one child?

Yes. Our team will work with you so that each sponsorship supports each child's needs and goals.

Will I hear how the child is doing?

Yes. Sponsors receive regular updates on their child's progress and the difference their support makes.

Do I need coaching experience to volunteer?

No. A love for sport and for young people is enough. We will find the right role for you.

Can I visit?

Yes. Contact us to arrange a visit to the academy in Kitende, off Entebbe Road.

A home, a school place and a family for talented children. An initiative of Star Hearts Sports Academy, Kitende.
